Two 'til Two

What it to do to die today, at a minute or two 'til two;

a thing distinctly hard to say, yet harder still to do.

To beat a tattoo, at a quarter to two,

a rat-tat-tat- tat-tat-tat- tat-tat-tattoo.

And the dragon will come when he hears the drum,

at a minute or two 'til two today, at a minute or two 'til two.
